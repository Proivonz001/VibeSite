## What it is

EffeUno turns the raw telemetry that Formula 1 publishes after every session into a replay you can watch. Pick a race, a sprint or a qualifying since 2018 and the app reconstructs it from the positional data: every car on the track map, gaps and intervals updating lap by lap, sector blocks in TV colours, tyre age, pit stops, penalties, and the race-control and team-radio feed synced to the replay clock.

## Features

- **Animated replay** on a canvas track map, with interpolation between telemetry samples, zoom and pan, playback up to 30x, DRS zones and detection points derived from the data, marshal sectors and the pit lane.
- **Live-timing leaderboard** that freezes correctly at the flag: gaps, last and best lap, tyre wheel and age, pit count with durations, track-limit strikes, overtake and DRS indicators.
- **Engineering charts**: race pace box plot, average gap, tyre strategies, stint degradation with regression, pit times by team, top speeds, lap chart, gaps between any two drivers.
- **Lap comparison** of up to three laps: speed, throttle and brake traces, plus the cumulative time delta by distance.

## How it works

A FastAPI backend wraps the FastF1 library behind a data-source interface, so a live-timing source can be plugged in later without a rewrite. Sessions load in a background thread and the browser polls until the payload is ready. The React front end computes standings, charts and the feed client-side from a single replay payload.

No F1 data is stored in the repository or on the demo site. The public demo runs on a synthetic race with fictional drivers, teams and circuit generated by a script; to watch real sessions you run the app locally and FastF1 downloads the data to your disk.

## How it was built

Planned and written with Claude Code, starting from a data-exploration phase on real sessions before any UI existed. The hardest part was not the charts but the edge cases: sprint shootouts, races without a fastest lap, sessions with missing upstream data. Each one is handled with a clear message instead of a cryptic error.

## Status

Released and usable. Live timing is designed for but not implemented.
